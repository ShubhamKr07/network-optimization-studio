## Prioritized recommendations

### 1. Investigate `super-model`: 10 solves triggered, 0 completed, and no recorded failure (high)
- `super-model` shows 5 scenarios created and 10 solves triggered, but 0 completed.
- `failuresByModel` and `rejectionsByModel` are both empty. The solves are therefore neither completing nor being recorded as failed or rejected.
- Recommendation: find out why these solves leave no outcome event. Possible causes include a missing failure event, abandoned or in-flight jobs, or an unregistered model id. The aggregates cannot tell which.
- Recommendation: make sure every triggered solve ends in exactly one of completed, failed or rejected, so this gap becomes visible.
- Open question: I cannot tell from the aggregates whether `super-model` is a real or test model id. It is worth confirming.

### 2. Reconcile the `p-median-us` funnel counts (high, data quality)
- `solveCompleted` (112) is higher than `solveTriggered` (93), and both are close to `created` (103).
- A completed count above the triggered count suggests the events are not counted on the same basis. For example, they may be counted per event rather than per unique scenario, or the weekly windows may differ.
- Recommendation: define each funnel step over the same population, such as unique scenarios in the week, before using it to judge drop-off.

### 3. Export is the largest observed drop-off (medium)
- For `p-median-us`, only 1 export followed 112 completed solves.
- Recommendation: check whether export is discoverable after a solve completes (placement, prompt, or a result-page call to action). It may also simply be a low-need action for students. The aggregates do not distinguish these.
- Recommendation: track export-button views or clicks so that "not seen" can be told apart from "not wanted".

### 4. Stale-without-resolve behaviour is low-signal (low)
- `staleResolveRate` is about 1.9%. The aggregates do not define the numerator and denominator.
- Recommendation: document the metric definition alongside the aggregate. Then check whether the rate means that stale scenarios are mostly re-solved or mostly left stale. Without that definition, no product change is justified yet.

### 5. Runtime and cache metrics look unpopulated (low, data quality)
- `runtimeP50`, `runtimeP95` and `cacheHitRate` are all 0, even though 112 solves are reported complete.
- A zero runtime is implausible for a real CBC solve. This looks like an instrumentation or reporting gap rather than a measurement.
- Recommendation: confirm that runtime and cache properties are emitted and aggregated. Until then, do not draw performance conclusions.
