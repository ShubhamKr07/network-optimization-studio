# Weekly Product Insights — week ending 2026-09-21

Source: `docs/product-insights/aggregates.json` (aggregate counts only, no PII). Every claim below is derived directly from that file; where the data is ambiguous or insufficient to draw a conclusion, that is stated explicitly rather than inferred.

## Summary

Activity this week was low: 6 scenarios were created and 10 solves were triggered, across only 3 of the app's models (`p-median-us`, `chens-cosmetics-cn`, `two-echelon-jade-us`). No exports were recorded for any model. No solve failures or precheck rejections were recorded. Given the small volume, treat every observation below as directional, not statistically reliable — see the "low priority" section for scope caveats.

## Findings, most actionable first

### 1. Zero exports across every active model (High priority)
`exported: 0` for all three models with activity, despite a combined 10 completed solves this week (`p-median-us`: 1, `chens-cosmetics-cn`: 5, `two-echelon-jade-us`: 5). That is a 100% drop-off at the export step wherever a solve happened.

**Recommendation:** Don't assume this means the export feature is undiscovered or broken — at this volume it could simply mean nobody needed to export yet. But it's worth a cheap check: confirm the export buttons are reachable from the tabs students actually land on after a solve, and consider instrumenting *which* export entity/format is clicked (even if the click doesn't complete) to tell "never opened the export UI" apart from "opened it and abandoned it." Re-evaluate once weekly created-scenario volume is higher — a single low-activity week isn't enough to call this a real funnel problem yet.

### 2. `chens-cosmetics-cn` solveCompleted (5) exceeds solveTriggered (4) (High priority — data quality)
For `chens-cosmetics-cn`, `solveCompleted` is 5 but `solveTriggered` is only 4. A completed-solve count can't exceed the number of triggers within a self-consistent weekly window, so this is either a boundary/windowing artifact (e.g. a solve triggered in a prior week completing inside this week's window) or a double-count in the aggregation pipeline for this specific model.

**Recommendation:** Before trusting any per-model funnel comparison from this pipeline, have engineering audit how `solveTriggered`/`solveCompleted` are windowed and counted for `chens-cosmetics-cn` specifically (it's the only model exhibiting the anomaly this week — `two-echelon-jade-us` is 5/5 and `p-median-us` is 1/1, both internally consistent).

### 3. `staleResolveRate: 0` is not interpretable on its own (High priority — instrumentation gap)
The aggregate reports a single ratio, `staleResolveRate: 0`, with no accompanying count of how many scenarios actually went stale this week. A value of 0 is consistent with two very different situations: (a) no scenario went stale at all (nothing to resolve — benign), or (b) scenarios went stale and none of them were ever re-solved (a real "stale-without-resolve" problem, which is explicitly one of the things this report is supposed to flag). The current data cannot distinguish these.

**Recommendation:** Emit a raw numerator/denominator pair (e.g. `staleCount` and `staleResolvedCount`) alongside the rate in future aggregates, so a future report can actually tell "no stale scenarios" apart from "stale scenarios nobody came back to fix."

### 4. Failures and rejections both empty — good, but unverified (Medium priority)
`failuresByModel: {}` and `rejectionsByModel: {}` are both empty, meaning zero solve failures and zero precheck rejections were recorded this week across all models. Taken at face value this is a healthy signal, but an empty object is also what you'd see if the failure/rejection events simply aren't being fired or captured — the aggregate can't distinguish "zero occurrences" from "not instrumented."

**Recommendation:** Spot-check that a deliberately-triggered failure (e.g. an infeasible scenario) actually produces a `failuresByModel`/`rejectionsByModel` entry in this pipeline, so an all-zero week can be trusted as a real reliability signal going forward rather than a silent instrumentation gap.

### 5. Runtime percentiles and cache hit rate are all zero (Medium priority — likely low-volume artifact)
`runtimeP50: 0`, `runtimeP95: 0`, and `cacheHitRate: 0` despite 10 recorded solve triggers this week. At only 10 solves, this is plausibly just an underpopulated or not-yet-wired metric rather than a genuine "every solve took 0 seconds and never hit cache" result.

**Recommendation:** Don't act on these numbers yet. Confirm they're actually being populated from real solve-job timing/cache data (not defaulting to 0 when the sample is small), then revisit once weekly solve volume is higher.

## Lower priority / no action needed

- `p-median-us` (1 created → 1 triggered → 1 completed) and `two-echelon-jade-us` (5 triggered → 5 completed, against 1 created scenario, consistent with normal iterative re-solving of the same scenario) both show healthy, internally consistent funnels with no drop-off signal.
- Three models present in the codebase — `transport-coal`, `p-median-brazil`, `two-echelon-gold-au` — have no entry at all in `funnelsByModel`, implying zero created/solved scenarios for them this week. With only 6 total scenarios created app-wide, this is consistent with a low-traffic pilot week rather than a per-model problem, and isn't flagged as an issue on its own.

## Overall recommendation

Given how low this week's volume is (6 scenarios, 10 solves, 3 of the app's models touched at all), the most valuable next step is closing the instrumentation gaps above (items 2, 3, 4) so that once weekly volume grows, the funnel/failure/staleness numbers can actually be trusted and compared week over week. No product or UX change is warranted from this week's data alone.
