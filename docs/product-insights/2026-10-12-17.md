## Prioritized recommendations

### 1. Fix or verify runtime and cache instrumentation (high priority, low effort)
`runtimeP50` and `runtimeP95` are both 0, and `cacheHitRate` is 0, even though 579 solves completed. A 0 ms solve is implausible for a CBC solve. This is most likely an event property that is not being emitted or aggregated. It could also mean the metrics are not collected at all. I cannot tell which from the aggregates.
- Check that the solve-completed event carries a duration, and that the aggregation reads it.
- Until this is fixed, we cannot assess solve performance, and the zero cache hit rate cannot be interpreted.

### 2. Investigate the created → exported drop-off on `two-echelon-jade-us` (high priority)
There were 208 scenarios created and 557 solves completed, but only 1 export. Export is effectively unused.
- The file does not say whether students need to export, or whether the export entry point is hard to find. Check the UI placement and discoverability of export before changing anything.
- If export is meant to be a key step in the learning flow, consider prompting it after a completed solve.

### 3. Understand the stale-without-resolve behavior (medium priority)
`staleResolveRate` is about 8.3%. The file does not define whether higher or lower is better, so first confirm the metric definition. If it is the share of stale scenarios that get re-solved, then about 92% of stale scenarios are left unresolved, and a clearer stale badge or a "re-solve" call to action would be worth testing. If it is the opposite, no action is needed.
- Do not act on this until the definition is confirmed.

### 4. Reconcile the funnel event counts (medium priority)
Two counts do not fit a strict funnel:
- `two-echelon-jade-us` has more completed solves (557) than triggered solves (532).
- `p-median-us` has 0 triggered solves but 1 completed solve.

Completed solves exceeding triggered solves suggests solves are started by a path that does not emit the trigger event, such as re-runs, retries, or async job recovery. Alternatively, events may fall in different week windows. Audit where `solveTriggered` is emitted so the funnel can be trusted.

### 5. Note the low adoption of `max-coverage-us` and `p-median-us` (low priority)
`max-coverage-us` has 1 scenario created and 22 solves. `p-median-us` has 4 scenarios created and 1 completed solve. Samples this small support no conclusions. Keep collecting data. The activity is consistent with the other models being used but not widely.

### 6. Solve failures and rejections: no action
No failures or rejections were recorded for any model. Keep monitoring. An empty map could also mean failure events are not instrumented, so it is worth confirming that failure events can be emitted (see the same caution in recommendation 1).
