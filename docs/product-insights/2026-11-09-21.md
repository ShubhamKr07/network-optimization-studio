## Prioritized recommendations

### 1. Investigate `super-model`: 4 created, 0 solves (highest priority)
`super-model` is the only model with a funnel drop-off: 4 scenarios created and none triggered a solve. Every other model with creations triggered at least as many solves as it created. Its absence from the seven models listed in CLAUDE.md is a possible explanation, but the aggregates don't say. Recommendation:
- Check what `super-model` is. It may be a test or placeholder id, or an unregistered or locked model.
- If it is a real user-facing model, find out why users never reach a solve. Possible causes are a missing dataset, a locked capability, or a confusing setup step.
- If it is not user-facing, exclude it from the funnel so it doesn't distort the numbers.

### 2. Fix the p-median-us event counts: completed (8) exceeds triggered (6)
Completions exceeding triggers is not possible in a clean funnel for one week. Likely causes are:
- Solves triggered before the week began that finished inside it.
- Solve-trigger events not firing on some paths, such as retries or re-solves.
- Completions counted per job where triggers are counted per scenario.

Until this is resolved, p-median-us funnel rates can't be trusted. Recommendation: audit where `solveTriggered` and `solveCompleted` are emitted and make their counting units match.

### 3. Verify runtime and cache telemetry
Solves completed this week (13 across models), yet `runtimeP50`, `runtimeP95` and `cacheHitRate` are all 0. A real solve cannot take 0 ms. This looks like missing instrumentation or an aggregation bug, not real behaviour. Recommendation: confirm runtime and cache fields are populated in the source events and in the aggregation query. Without them we can't see slow solves, which are the main solve-failure precursor.

### 4. Export is the weakest funnel step
Only 2 exports occurred (one each for p-median-us and max-coverage-us) against 13 completed solves. delivery-teaching-us and two-echelon-jade-us had solves but 0 exports. With this volume it may just be normal, since students may read results in the UI. Recommendation: no change yet. Re-check once more weeks accumulate, and if export stays low consider a clearer export prompt on the results view.

### 5. Stale-without-resolve: no signal

Recommendation: add the stale-scenario count as a denominator to the aggregate so this rate can be interpreted. No product change is justified from this data.
